A lighter way into AI alignment, built for Southeast Asia.

Odysea is a free, 8 week pilot for anyone in SEA curious about AI safety: weekday evenings, topics shaped by the group, sessions led by local TAs and grounded in Iliad's curriculum. No cost, no fixed syllabus, no prerequisites beyond curiosity.

How to join

What we'll cover

An Iliad curriculum, adapted for Southeast Asia

Odysea isn't Iliad's course run unchanged: it's been reshaped, in consultation with Iliad, into a lighter, more introductory on-ramp for the region. Rather than assuming prior technical background, sessions are designed to build genuine interest in AI safety and lay solid groundwork in alignment fundamentals. Each week, local TAs draw on Iliad's course material as a shared pool and pick what the cohort is ready to go deeper on, with input from Iliad's advisors on sequencing and rigor. The clusters below are illustrative starting points, not a locked in schedule.

Format

3 hours, one evening a week, for 8 weeks

Sessions run on weekday evenings, Singapore time, exact day confirmed with each cohort. Each session blends a short introduction to that week's topic with guided discussion and hands on work, wrapping up with a look ahead to the following week. Lightweight by design: come for one session, or all eight.

The model behind this

Why we think this works: TARA's track record

Odysea doesn't have outcomes of its own yet: this is our first pilot. But the underlying model, a part time, locally adapted variant of an established curriculum, has already been tested in the region. TARA adapted ARENA the same way Odysea adapts Iliad's curriculum, and its first cohort's results are the closest thing we have to a track record.

Team

A Singapore-based team, building for Southeast Asia

Odysea is organized by a small core team, with weekly sessions led by local TAs who curate from Iliad's syllabus.

Core team

Teaching Assistants

Our TAs typically come in at Master's or PhD level, with hands on exposure to AI safety, not necessarily career researchers, but seriously engaged with the field.

Advisors

Guidance from Iliad, and beyond

Iliad advises on how the curriculum translates to Southeast Asia, helping keep topic selection pedagogically sound and technically grounded. Alongside them, a small group of external advisors bring additional perspective from the region and the field. Day to day delivery sits entirely with the local team.
