Format

3 hours, one evening a week, for 8 weeks

Sessions run on weekday evenings, Singapore time.

The model behind this

Modeled on TARA's track record, with one key difference

Odysea has no outcomes of its own yet, as our first pilot, but the underlying model does: TARA adapted ARENA into a part-time, locally adapted program and measurably widened who enters the field. TARA's cohorts leaned implementation-focused; Odysea tests whether the same format works for a more theoretical, math/CS-leaning introduction, since Southeast Asia's pipeline doesn't have one yet.

Source: TARA's Sydney/Melbourne 2025 pilot cohort, based on a survey of 15 of 19 graduates. Full report ↗
