AI Safety · Singapore · Pilot Cohort

A lighter way into AI alignment, built for Singapore.

Odysea is a free, 8 week pilot for anyone curious about AI safety: weekday evenings, topics shaped by the group, sessions led by local TAs and grounded in Iliad's curriculum. No cost, no fixed syllabus, no prerequisites beyond curiosity.

Express interest Join the community

Free to join. This is Singapore's first OdySEA pilot, community curated, TA led, and advised by Iliad.

How to join

Two ways in, pick your level of commitment

Both tracks run on the same weekly sessions and the same curriculum. The difference is structure, not content, and there's no cost either way.

Low commitment

Explorer

For anyone still figuring out their interest in AI safety. Come to whichever sessions catch your eye.

  • Drop into any individual session
  • No attendance requirement
  • No penalty for missing a week
  • No capstone required
Full commitment

Fellow

For those ready to commit to the full pilot and walk away with a project to show for it.

  • Express interest upfront
  • Attend at least 75% of sessions (max 3 absences across 8 weeks)
  • Complete a short capstone project
  • Certificate of completion

What we'll cover

Shaped by the room, grounded in Iliad's curriculum

There's no fixed syllabus set months in advance. Each week, local TAs, drawing on Iliad's course material as a shared pool, pick what the cohort most wants to go deep on, with input from Iliad's advisors on sequencing and rigor. The clusters below are illustrative of what's on the table, not a locked in schedule.

Agent Foundations & Decision Theory

How to formally reason about agents, their goals, and the decisions they make.

Learning Theory & Generalization

Why learned systems behave the way they do, and where that reasoning breaks down.

Interpretability

Looking inside models to understand what they're actually representing and doing.

Value Alignment & Corrigibility

Getting systems to reliably pursue what we actually want, and stay correctable if they don't.

Robustness & Evaluations

Stress testing systems and building ways to measure whether they're actually safe.

AI Governance & Strategy

The policy, coordination, and strategic landscape around advanced AI.

Format

3 hours, one evening a week, for 8 weeks

Sessions run on weekday evenings, Singapore time, exact day confirmed with each cohort. Each session blends a short introduction to that week's topic with guided discussion and hands on work, wrapping up with a look ahead to the following week. Lightweight by design: come for one session, or all eight.

The model behind this

Why we think this works: TARA's track record

Odysea doesn't have outcomes of its own yet: this is our first pilot. But the underlying model, a part time, locally adapted variant of an established curriculum, has already been tested in the region. TARA adapted ARENA the same way Odysea adapts Iliad's curriculum, and its first cohort's results are the closest thing we have to a track record.

90% likelihood to recommend the program
91% more likely to pursue an AI safety career
40% secured a competitive fellowship within 12 months
47% transitioned into an AI safety role within 12 months

Source: TARA's Sydney/Melbourne 2025 pilot cohort, based on a survey of 15 of 19 graduates. Full report ↗

Who it's for

Built for the AI curious, not just AI researchers
